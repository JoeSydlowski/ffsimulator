Playoff Odds

How to read this

Playoff / Champ
Share of simulated seasons that make the playoffs / win the title. Only the games left are simulated, from live FantasyPros rest-of-season ranks on the real schedule; the record already banked is added before seeding (wins, then points-for), with this league’s number of berths.

Change since the previous run of this report.
Seeds
Each cell is one final seed, darker = likelier. The rule after a cell marks the playoff cut.
Posture
Contender (playoff 65%+ or title odds 1.5× a fair share), Bubble (30–65%), and below that Seller (an older-than-median roster in a dynasty or keeper league — the team with veterans to move), Rebuilding (young roster), or Long shot (redraft). Early in a season this mostly restates roster strength.
Need
The position where a team’s simulated starters score furthest below the league median, in points per week (at least 1 point below counts).
Targets / Pitches
Targets: veterans (past a positional age line — RB 26, WR 28, TE 29, QB 32 — or priced 15%+ higher for this season than the long run, and worth at least 500 redraft) on teams that are out of it. Pitches: your veterans, and the contenders whose need they fill. The list that suits your posture in the league comes first.
Not modelled
Known multi-week absences are treated as random missed weeks, and teams fill holes only from their own benches (no waiver streaming).
